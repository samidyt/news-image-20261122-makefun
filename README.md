sum up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026 in one image.

## The image

A 6-panel grid (1024×683 PNG) with a "22 NOVEMBER 2026 — THE WORLD IN
ONE DAY" banner. The six stories, all events that happened or were first
reported on 22 November 2026 (or dominated that day's news cycle):

1. **COP31 in overtime** — in Antalya, the final draft released early
   Sunday drops all mention of fossil fuels; talks run into a second day
   of overtime, with a plenary on Sunday morning to approve a deal.
2. **Gulf flashpoint** — Iran strikes a civilian industrial site in
   Bahrain late Saturday (fire, no injuries); two tankers are hit in the
   Strait of Hormuz; the Revolutionary Guards claim US bases in Bahrain
   targeted. Reported and updated across Sunday's news cycle.
3. **AI exam cheat** — South Korea's top tax-exam scorer admits in a
   video published Sunday that he used a micro-camera and AI (the
   111-candidate Suneung smart-device crackdown was announced Friday).
4. **India opens Nitish Setu** — Modi inaugurates the bridge linking
   Patna and Vaishali; India–Japan defence cooperation deepens.
5. **Robot weapons pact** — states agree at UN talks to create an
   international pact on autonomous weapons.
6. **Alcaraz v Zverev** — the ATP Finals title decider.

No recognizable faces of living people appear; the exam-taker is a generic
illustration seen from behind. No graphic violence.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) copies the pre-built `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and verifies it is a PNG under
1 MiB. No network, no randomness — every run writes the same bytes.
